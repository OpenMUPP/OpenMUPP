OCD register experiments on the ATmega2560 (OCD revision 4), PICkit 5 driven one script at a time.
Each block is one debug session. "@ocd" reads all 16 OCD registers (IR 0xB). Values are register data (bits 15..0).
"@save" reads PC, r0..r31 and EIND after a stop. PC values are word addresses.
Test program: step_me at word 0xCC, main at word 0xA3, counter16 at data address 0x210.
